# Project Cost Analysis

## Major Formulas
---
$$
\boxed{ 
\Pi_{\text{net}} = 
\underbrace{
    \underbrace{ 
        \underbrace{ 
            R\_{\text{op}} - C\_{\text{COGS}} 
        }\_{\Pi\_{\text{gross}}} - 
        C\_{\text{OpEx}} 
    }\_{\Pi\_{\text{EBIT}}} + 
    R\_{\text{non-op}} - 
    C\_{\text{non-op}}
 }\_{\Pi\_{EBT}} - C\_{\text{tax}} 
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{Gross Profit Margin} = 
\tfrac{
    \Pi\_{\text{gross}}
}{
    R\_{\text{op}}
} =
\tfrac{
    R\_{\text{op}} - C\_{\text{COGS}}
}{
    R\_{\text{op}}
}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{Operation Profit Margin} = 
\tfrac{
    \Pi\_{\text{EBIT}}
}{
    R\_{\text{op}}
}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{Net Profit Margin} = 
\tfrac{
    \Pi\_{\text{net}}
}{
    R\_{\text{op}}
}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
C\_{\text{unit}} = 
\tfrac{
    C\_{\text{COGS}}
    }{
    Q
}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
P\_{\text{unit}} = 
\tfrac{
    R\_{\text{op}}
    }{
    Q
}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
\text{Gross Profit Per Unit} = 
P\_{\text{unit}} - C\_{\text{unit}} = 
\tfrac{
    \Pi\_{\text{gross}}
}{Q}
}
$$

$$
\boxed{
   \text{Markup} =
   \tfrac{
P\_{\text{unit}} - C\_{\text{unit}}
   }{C\_{\text{unit}}}
}
\quad
\therefore
\quad
\boxed{
P\_{\text{unit}} = C\_{\text{unit}} \cdot (1 + \text{Markup})
}
$$

## Terminology
$$
\Pi_{\text{Net}} - \text{Net Profit} \newline
\Pi_{\text{Gross}} - \text{Gross Profit} \newline
\Pi_{\text{EBIT}} - \text{Earnings Before Interest and Taxes} \newline
\Pi_{\text{EBT}} - \text{Earnings Before Tax} \newline
R_{\text{op}} - \text{Operating Revenue} \newline
C_{\text{COGS}} - \text{Cost of Goods Sold} \newline
C_{\text{OpEx}} - \text{Operating Expenses} \newline
R_{\text{non-op}} - \text{Non-Operating Income} \newline
C_{\text{non-op}} - \text{Non-Operating Expenses} \newline
$$

---
Operational revenue $R_{\text{op}}$:
- product revenue
- services revenue
- contract revenue
- rental & Leasing revenue
- subscription revenue
- commission revenue
- commission & brokerage revenue
- licensing & royalty revenue

---
Cost of Goods Sold $C\_{\text{COGS}}$:
- materials (e.g. direct, raw)
- merchandise purchased for resale
- direct production labor
- labor involved in delivering a service
- production/assembly costs
- transportation/freight directly related to acquiring goods
- import duties and production-related taxes
- other costs directly attributable to producing or acquiring the goods sold

---
Operating expenses $C\_{\text{OpEx}}$:
- salaries and wages (e.g. administrative, sales, management, office staff)
- accounting expenses
- license fees
- maintenance and repairs (e.g. snow, trash removal, janitorial services)
- advertising and marketing
- office expenses
- office supplies
- legal and attorney fees
- utilities (e.g. telephone, internet, electricity)
- insurance
- property management expenses
- property taxes
- travel expenses
- vehicle expenses — when not directly attributable to production/delivery
- leasing commissions
- depreciation and amortization (e.g. company vehicle, licenses, patents)

---
Operational revenue $R_{\text{non-op}}$:
- interest income
- dividend income
- gain on sale of investments
- gain on sale of non-operating assets
- foreign-exchange gains
- gains on financial instruments
- other investment income
- other non-operating gains

---
Non-operating expenses $C_{\text{non-OpEx}}$:
- interest expense (e.g. debt interest)
- financing costs (e.g. back fees)
- foreign-exchange losses
- losses on financial instruments
- other investment losses

---
Income tax $C\_{\text{tax}}$:
- $\Pi\_{\text{EBT}}$ ; accounting profit before tax
- $ A\_{\text{tax}}$ ; non deductible expenses (e.g. fines, penalties)
- $ D\_{\text{tax}}$ ; deductible expenses (e.g. business travel, depreciation, operational cost, 
[Slovenia-income-tax-deductions law](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovenia/corporate/deductions))
- $t$ income tax rate
$$
C\_\text{tax} = t \cdot ( \Pi_{\text{EBT}} + A\_{\text{tax}} - D\_{\text{tax}})
$$
